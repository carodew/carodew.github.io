About

I’m a principal designer and researcher working in civic tech. I turn ambiguous, high-stakes problems into actionable, evidence-based strategy and direction. I use progressive de-risking to explore and vet new ideas. I specialize in streamlining complex business processes, making products accessible, and conducting inclusive design and research.

As a generalist I’m comfortable doing qualitative and quantitative research from discovery to evaluation, and designing complex end-to-end flows to detailed interaction design.
